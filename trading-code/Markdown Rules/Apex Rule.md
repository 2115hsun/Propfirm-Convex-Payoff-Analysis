# Apex Trader Funding — Evaluation & Payout Rules



_Source: apextraderfunding.com Help Center. Rules subject to change — verify on the official site._

Apex offers two paths: **EOD (End-of-Day) Trailing Drawdown** and **Intraday Trailing Drawdown**. Passing an evaluation activates a **Performance Account (PA)** (Simulated Funded).

## 1. EOD Trailing Drawdown Path
### EOD Evaluation — How to Pass
- 30-day access; EOD drawdown calculated once/day at close, fixed and enforced next session.
- No minimum trading days (can pass in one day). 7 calendar days to activate PA.
- To pass: reach profit target AND never touch/fall below the EOD Drawdown level.

| Parameter | 25K | 50K | 100K | 150K |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Profit Target | $1,500 | $3,000 | $6,000 | $9,000 |
| Max Drawdown (EOD) | $1,000 | $2,000 | $3,000 | $4,000 |
| Daily Loss Limit | $500 | $1,000 | $1,500 | $2,000 |
| Max Contracts | 4 | 6 | 8 | 12 |
| Access Period | 30 Days | 30 Days | 30 Days | 30 Days |
| Consistency | Not applied | Not applied | Not applied | Not applied |

### EOD Payouts
- Up to weekly, 100% split. Min 5 qualifying trading days (not necessarily consecutive). 50% consistency rule. Min payout $500. Max 6 payouts/PA.

| Account | Min Days | Min Daily Profit | Safety Net | Min Balance to Request | Max Payouts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | 5 | $100 | $26,100 | $26,600 | 6 |
| 50K | 5 | $250 | $52,100 | $52,600 | 6 |
| 100K | 5 | $300 | $103,100 | $103,600 | 6 |
| 150K | 5 | $350 | $154,100 | $154,600 | 6 |

Safety Net = drawdown limit + $100; only profit above it is withdrawable.
Max Payout Per Request (EOD): 25K $1,000 all payouts; 50K $1,500→$3,000; 100K $2,000→$4,000; 150K $2,500→$5,000 (rising by payout #1–6).

## 2. Intraday Trailing Drawdown Path
### Intraday Evaluation — How to Pass
- Real-time trailing drawdown follows Peak Balance (incl. unrealized) and never moves down.
- No Daily Loss Limit; no minimum days; 7 days to activate PA.
- To pass: reach profit target AND never breach the intraday trailing drawdown.

| Parameter | 25K | 50K | 100K | 150K |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Profit Target | $1,500 | $3,000 | $6,000 | $9,000 |
| Max Drawdown (Intraday) | $1,000 | $2,000 | $3,000 | $4,000 |
| Max Contracts | 4 | 6 | 8 | 12 |

Intraday PA (funded): trailing stops once Max Drawdown + $100 reached; DLL intraday; tier scaling. PA max contracts: 2/4/6/10.

### Intraday Payouts
- Up to weekly, 100% split. Min 5 qualifying days. 50% consistency. Min $500. Max 6 payouts.

| Account | Min Days | Min Daily Profit | Safety Net | Min Balance to Request |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | 5 | $100 | $26,100 | $26,600 |
| 50K | 5 | $200 | $52,100 | $52,600 |
| 100K | 5 | $250 | $103,100 | $103,600 |
| 150K | 5 | $300 | $154,100 | $154,600 |

After requesting a payout you may keep trading, but trade as if funds already removed; request auto-denied if balance falls below the required minimum.
