# Lucid Trading (LucidPro) — Evaluation & Payout Rules

_Source: support.lucidtrading.com. Rules subject to change — verify on the official site._

## Evaluation — How to Pass
Simulated account with a profit target that must be hit to upgrade to a funded account. One-time fee, no rebilling. Can pass in one trading day. Real-time activation (~5–30 min). No activation fees.

| Account | Profit Target | Max Loss Limit | Daily Loss Limit | Max Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $25,000 | $1,250 | $1,000 | None | 2 mini / 20 micro |
| $50,000 | $3,000 | $2,000 | $1,200 | 4 mini / 40 micro |
| $100,000 | $6,000 | $3,000 | $1,800 | 6 mini / 60 micro |
| $150,000 | $9,000 | $4,500 | $2,700 | 10 mini / 100 micro |

Drawdown type: EOD (End of Day).

## Payout Rules (Funded)
- Requests are final once submitted. Profit split 90% trader / 10% Lucid (accounts before 11/28/2025 3PM EST keep 100% on first $10k).
- All conditions below must be met; they reset after each approved payout.

1) Minimum Profit Goal/cycle: $250 (25K) / $500 (50K) / $750 (100K) / $1,000 (150K).
2) Consistency: largest single-day profit ≤ 40% of total profit in the cycle.
3) Profit over Buffer (= Initial Max Loss Limit + $100): $26,100 (25K) / $52,100 (50K) / $103,100 (100K) / $154,600 (150K).

Min payout request: $500. Max: 25K $1,000→$1,500; 50K $2,000→$2,500; 100K $2,500→$3,000; 150K $3,000→$3,500 (Payout 1 → 2+).

Timing: request any day after criteria met; on approval funds deducted within minutes, disbursed within 2 business days.
