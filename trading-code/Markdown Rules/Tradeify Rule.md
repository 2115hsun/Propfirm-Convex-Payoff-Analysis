# Tradeify — Evaluation & Payout Rules

_Source: help.tradeify.co (Tradeify 3.0). Rules subject to change — verify on the official site._

Two main evaluation types: Select and Growth. Sizes: $25K/$50K/$100K/$150K.

## 1. SELECT Evaluation — How to Pass
- Minimum 3 trading days (due to consistency). 40% consistency (eval only): no single day > 40% of total profit. No Daily Loss Limit during eval. EOD drawdown. T1 news allowed; no activation fees.

| Account | Profit Target | Max Drawdown (EOD) | DLL | Consistency | Max Contracts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | $1,500 | $1,000 | None | 40% | 1 mini / 10 micro |
| 50K | $3,000 | $2,000 | None | 40% | 4 mini / 40 micro |
| 100K | $6,000 | $3,000 | None | 40% | 8 mini / 80 micro |
| 150K | $9,000 | $4,500 | None | 40% | 12 mini / 120 micro |

After passing choose Select Flex (5-Day) or Select Daily (permanent per account).
- Select Flex: payout every 5 winning days; up to 50% of profits (caps $1,250/$3,000/$4,000/$5,000); no DLL; EOD drawdown.
- Select Daily: daily eligibility after buffer; caps $600/$1,000/$1,500/$2,500; DLL $500/$1,000/$1,250/$1,750; buffer $1,100/$2,100/$2,600/$3,600.
- 40% consistency applies only during evaluation (removed when funded).

## 2. GROWTH Evaluation — How to Pass
- Can pass in as few as 1 day (no consistency). Has a Daily Loss Limit (soft breach) — stops trading for the day, does not fail account. EOD Trailing Max Drawdown. No time limit.

| Account | Profit Target | DLL (soft) | Trailing Max Drawdown | Max Position Size |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 25K | $1,500 | $600 | $1,000 | 1 contract (10 micros) |
| 50K | $3,000 | $1,250 | $2,000 | 4 contracts (40 micros) |
| 100K | $6,000 | $2,500 | $3,500 | 8 contracts (80 micros) |
| 150K | $9,000 | $3,750 | $5,000 | 12 contracts (120 micros) |

Growth Sim Funded consistency: 35% (no single day > 35% of total profit before payout; resets each payout).

### Growth Funded Payout Policy
- 90% split. Max 5 total sim funded accounts. 35% consistency each payout.
- Min 5 trading days each showing profit > $100 (25K) / $150 (50K) / $200 (100K) / $250 (150K); day count resets after each payout.
- Min balance needed: $26,500 / $53,000 / $104,500 / $156,500.
- Min payout: $250 / $500 / $1,000 / $1,500. Max caps rise by payout #: 25K $1,000; 50K $1,500→$3,000; 100K $2,000→$4,000; 150K $2,500→$5,000.
- On APPROVED, funds withdrawn immediately, paid within 24–48h (up to 72h off-hours).

## Funding After Passing
- Instant activation; no activation fee (Growth/Select). Funded account starts at base size; evaluation profits do NOT carry over.
