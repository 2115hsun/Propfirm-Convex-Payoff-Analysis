# Economics

- Eval stage: attempts $= 1/p_e$, cost $=$ attempts × fee, days $=$ (attempts − 1) × fail days + pass days.
- Payouts per funded account: $q / (1 - p)$, a geometric series.
- Cycle: net $=$ cash − eval cost, days $=$ eval days + funded days, ROI $=$ net / eval cost.
