# Time layer

Expected trades until payout or breach under a fixed policy:

$$T(s) = 1 + \sum_{s'} P(s' \mid s)\, T(s')$$

with $T = 0$ at both finish lines. No max: it measures the chosen policy. Pre-lock adds $T_{post}(x')$ at a lock.

Result at $250: 18.8 trades fresh to first payout or breach; 17.9 per post-payout cycle.
