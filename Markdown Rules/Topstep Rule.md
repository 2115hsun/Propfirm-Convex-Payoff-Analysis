# Topstep — Evaluation & Payout Rules

_Source: topstep.com & help.topstep.com. Rules subject to change — verify on the official site._

Path: Trading Combine® → Express Funded Account® (XFA) → Live Funded Account® (LFA). Sizes $50K/$100K/$150K.

## 1. Trading Combine® — How to Pass
Rule: do not let balance hit/go below the Maximum Loss Limit (MLL).
Objectives: (1) reach & maintain Profit Target; (2) meet Consistency Target — best single day below 50% of Profit Target (else required profit increases). Can pass in as few as 2 days.

| Account | Profit Target | Best Day (<50%) | Max Contracts | Max Micros |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $50K | $3,000 | < $1,500 | 5 | 50 |
| $100K | $6,000 | < $3,000 | 10 | 100 |
| $150K | $9,000 | < $4,500 | 15 | 150 |

Micros/minis 10:1. Consistency = Best Day ÷ Total Profit. Losses don't reset best day.

## 2. Express Funded Account® (XFA)
Starts at $0; up to 5 active XFAs. Choose Standard or Consistency path.
- Standard: payout eligibility = 5 winning days of $150+; max payout 50% of balance up to $5,000; 90/10.
- Consistency: 3 days traded + 40% consistency (largest day ÷ total net ≤ 40%); max payout 50% up to $6,000; 90/10.
Rule (both): don't hit MLL; follow Scaling Plan.

## 3. Payout Policy
- Via dashboard during CME hours. Min payout $125. 90/10 split.

XFA caps (max/request = 50% of balance up to cap):
| Account | Standard | Consistency |
|---|---|---|
| $50K | $2,000 | $3,000 |
| $100K | $3,000 | $4,000 |
| $150K | $5,000 | $6,000 |
(Voluntarily adding a Daily Loss Limit doubles the per-request cap.)

- XFA Standard: 5 winning days of $150+ (not necessarily consecutive; locks 4PM CT) AND positive net profit since last payout. After each payout MLL resets to $0, 5-day count restarts.
- XFA Consistency: 3 trading days (≥1 trade each) AND largest day ≤ 40% of total net. After each payout MLL resets to $0, consistency & 3-day count reset.
- Live Funded: 5 winning days of $150+/cycle; request up to 50% of balance, no dollar cap. After 30 winning days of $150+, unlock daily payouts (min $125, once/day).
- Methods: Prop-to-Brokerage (US, same day, no fee); Aeropay (US, instant); Wise (1–3 days); ACH (US, 1–3 days, $30); Wire/SWIFT (intl, 5–10 days, $30).
