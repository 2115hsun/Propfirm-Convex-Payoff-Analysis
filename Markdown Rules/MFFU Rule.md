# MyFundedFutures (MFFU) — Evaluation & Payout Rules

_Source: help.myfundedfutures.com. Rules subject to change — verify on the official site._

Plans: Rapid, Flex, Pro (and Builder). Sizes $25K/$50K/$100K/$150K. No activation fees.

## Evaluation — How to Pass
- EOD Drawdown trails positive EOD performance ($1 per $1). Intraday Drawdown (Rapid) trails equity to new highs, no decrease on losses.
- Consistency (eval): 50% — no single day > 50% of total eval profit (Rapid/Flex/Pro; Pro one-day pass has none). Exceeding it does NOT breach — just trade more days.
- Minimum trading days: 2.

Rapid $50K example: Profit Target $3,000; Max Loss (EOD) $2,000; DLL None; Max 5 mini/50 micro; Consistency 50% (eval only); Min 2 days; T1 news yes.
(Target ≈ 6% of account; EOD max loss ≈ 4% — e.g. 25K $1,500; 100K $6,000; 150K $9,000.)

## Sim Funded (Rapid example)
- Starts at $0. Max loss distance $2,000 from equity high-water mark (intraday trailing). Max loss LOCKS at $100; must keep ≥ $100 (below = breach). No consistency rule when funded. Max 5 mini/50 micro.

## Payout Rules
- Split: Rapid 90% (as of Jan 12, 2026); Flex & Pro 80%. Most payouts approved instantly (manual review 6–12h weekdays).
- Rapid: daily; first payout 24h after first trade; no consistency; min withdrawal $500 after buffer; buffer 50K $2,100 / 100K $3,100 / 150K $4,600.
- Flex: after 5 winning days (≥$100/day 25K, ≥$150/day 50K); min withdrawal $250; max per request 50% up to $3,000 (25K)/$5,000 (50K); after first payout Max Loss resets to $100.
- Pro (incl. 1-Day add-on): request every 14 calendar days from first trade; meet buffer (50K $2,100/100K $3,100/150K $4,600); can withdraw up to 60% of profits while in buffer; min withdrawal $1,000; max sim-funded request $100,000/user.
- Transition to Live: Rapid at $10k net profit in a single day (auto) or consistent payouts; Flex at 5 consecutive payouts or $100k sim cap; Pro at 3 consecutive payouts or $20k milestone.
- No daily loss limits on Flex, Rapid or Pro plans.
